Principles

What every piece here keeps, on qbix.space and off it.

  1. 01

    Uncover, never fade.

    Words rise out of sleeves, frames open on a clip, lime wipes in like a bar filling.

  2. 02

    The scroll is the playhead.

    Scenes play state by state as you scroll, and scrolling back plays them backwards.

  3. 03

    One lime a screen.

    A button, a live tag, the core of the cube, a marker. Never two at once.

  4. 04

    Blocks, never lines.

    Surfaces and 8px gaps tell things apart. No radii, no blur. The corner that matters is cut.

  5. 05

    Expo out to arrive.

    cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for an entrance, (0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for a move. Nothing bounces.

  6. 06

    Still when asked.

    Every piece rests for reduced motion, and drawings hold still while the page scrolls.
