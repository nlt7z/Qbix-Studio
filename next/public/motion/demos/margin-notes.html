Case study · Willow Counseling

A quiet site for a quiet room.

Willow Counseling is Dana Reyes’s practice in Seattle. Its old home page had nine blocks and no next step. We kept four and moved booking above the fold, where a new client already looks.

The old palette was grey on grey. We chose a calmer palette of warm paper and deep green ink, and held every pair at 7 to 1.

Type runs on one type scale: five sizes on one 8px grid. Dana adds a service page herself and it still looks like the rest.

Review took two rounds. The site went live in seven days, and new bookings went from 3 to 11 a month.
