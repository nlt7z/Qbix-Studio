Counseling that fits your week

Weekly sessions in Seattle and online, with evening slots and no waiting list.

Who we help

Adults and couples working through stress, grief and big changes. Most people start with a short intro call, then pick a counselor. Evenings and weekends are open.

How sessions work

You meet your counselor for 50 minutes, in person or by video. The first session is a conversation, not an intake form.

Pricing

Sessions are $120 for 50 minutes.
