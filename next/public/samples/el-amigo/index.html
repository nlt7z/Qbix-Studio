Since 1987

A taste of tradition, served with love

Since 1987, El Amigo Restaurant has been a place for authentic flavors, family traditions, and warm hospitality. For over 38 years, we’ve proudly served fresh, high-quality Mexican cuisine made with love.

Our success is thanks to our dedicated team and loyal customers who make it all possible. We’re grateful to be part of your dining experience and can’t wait to welcome you again!
