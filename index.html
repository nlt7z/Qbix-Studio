01 / ORIGIN

Why we started.

We wanted to make games that feel like small strange machines — easy to enter, hard to fully predict, satisfying to replay. The kind of thing you'd pick up on a Tuesday and still be thinking about on Friday.

02 / BELIEF

Small games can hold worlds.

A game doesn't need a hundred hours of content to feel like a place. The right system, the right rules, and a few well-chosen objects are enough. We'd rather build a tight 20-minute loop than a thin 20-hour one.

03 / METHOD

Prototype fast, polish hard.

We build playable prototypes in days, not months. If the loop doesn't feel right in week one, the world won't save it. Once a system clicks, we hold it to the same UI standard as a shipped product.

PAPER FIGMA WEB FIELD TEST
04 / TASTE

Tactical UI, strange systems.

We like games that respect the player's attention. Clean readable interfaces, mechanics that reward curiosity, worlds that are weirder than they first appear. Less neon, more dust.

05 / CURRENT FOCUS

What we're working on now.

Four projects in active development. BIDKING is in playable prototype — sharpening the auction tension and tuning the risk grid. PROJECT DUST and PROJECT SIGNAL are in concept and prototype phases. PROJECT VAULT is in closed internal testing.
